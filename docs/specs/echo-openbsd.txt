echo(1) - OpenBSD Man Page Flags
Source: https://man.openbsd.org/echo.1

-E    Disables interpretation of backslash escape
      sequences. This is the default behavior.

-e    Enables interpretation of backslash escape
      sequences, including: \\ (literal backslash),
      \a (alert/BEL), \b (backspace), \c (suppress
      remaining output and trailing newline),
      \e (escape character), \f (form feed),
      \n (newline), \r (carriage return),
      \t (horizontal tab), \v (vertical tab),
      \0nnn (character with octal value),
      \xhh (character with hexadecimal value).

-n    Prevents printing of the trailing newline
      character.
